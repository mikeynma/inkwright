The tape that listens: make your apprentice while it loads

Six minutes of loading become the start of the game. The loading screen arrives grey and its colours come home ink by ink; then a page opens beneath it and asks who you are. Type a name, take a keepsake from Orla's shelf, choose the colour of your cloak, while a candle burns down beside the page as the tape loads.

A game this size takes about six minutes from tape. INKWRIGHT spends them making you. The loading screen loads in grey, as the Vale is, and its colours come back one ink at a time. Then a page opens under the logo, and while the game's blocks keep loading behind it you type your name, take a keepsake from Orla's shelf (three tonics, two crow feathers, or her old steel nib) and choose your cloak's colour. A candle beside the page burns down as the tape goes: its wax is what is left to load, and its flame jumps with the data.

When the tape ends you walk straight in as yourself. Orla calls you by name, and your cloak is grey until you win its ink back.

How it works

Between the edges

The tape is an ordinary one at the ROM's speed: the stripes in the border are the real loading stripes. Our loader times every edge itself, and in the gap after each one it runs a small, fixed piece of the page: a key read, a letter drawn, a line of the candle. Every piece is the same length whichever way it goes, never more than 1,250 T-states, so no edge is ever missed. The page runs at 25 frames a second on what is left over.

From our Loader Lab

The loader comes from our Loader Lab, where the same trick first ran a little shooting game inside Shardstorm's loading screen. INKWRIGHT's page is its ninth experiment, S9.

Which emulator

An emulator that loads a tape by catching the ROM's loader and copying the bytes in can't run this one, because it is not the ROM reading the tape. Play it in real time in ZX Spin or Retro Virtual Machine, or on a real 128K. For the other kind there is the normal tape, inkwright-normal.tap: it loads through the ROM like any other game, and the game asks the same questions when you pick NEW JOURNEY.

How it was checked

The build loads the tape through our emulator's tape deck the way a person would, edge by edge, with no shortcut, and types a name, picks a keepsake and a cloak on the page while it loads. Every one of over 400,000 bits is timed and none is misread, with the tape played 6% fast and 6% slow as well. Then it presses NEW JOURNEY and checks the game took the choices. A second run is still choosing when the tape ends. Not yet tried on a real 128K.

Load it yourself

For the 128K. Three tapes, one game. inkwright-turbo.tzx lets you make your apprentice while it loads, in 3 minutes; inkwright-special.tap does the same at the ROM's own speed, in 6. Both must play in real time, as a cassette does (a real 128K, or ZX Spin or Retro Virtual Machine with fast loading off). inkwright-normal.tap loads the ordinary way and works in any emulator, even ones that load instantly; the game asks the same questions when you pick NEW JOURNEY. Not sure? Take the normal one.

  1. It needs a 128K (a 128K or a grey +2). Pick Tape Loader. On a 48K the tape says it needs a 128K and stops.
  2. Two tapes, one game: pick by how your emulator loads. Once you are playing they are exactly the same.
  3. The special tape, inkwright-special.tap, has the loading page: while it loads you type your name and choose a keepsake and a cloak, with a candle burning down beside you. The Spectrum's own loader can't do that, so the game reads the tape itself, and the tape has to play in real time, the way a cassette does. Use it on a real 128K, or in ZX Spin or Retro Virtual Machine with instant and fast loading switched off. It takes about six minutes.
  4. The normal tape, inkwright-normal.tap, loads the ordinary way, through the Spectrum's own loader, so it works in every emulator, including the ones that load a tape instantly (Fuse with fast loading, phone and web emulators). There is no loading page: when you pick NEW JOURNEY the game asks the same questions on its own screens. Not sure which? Take this one.
  5. Saving: at an inn, start the emulator's tape recording, then say yes. To carry on later, load that tape with Load from tape on the title.
  6. On a real Spectrum: play the tape into the EAR socket from a tape player or a TZX player.
