An original RPG for the 128K ZX Spectrum.

The Grey King has drunk the colour from the Vale. You are the last apprentice inkwright. Win back the inks, BLUE, RED and GREEN, at three shrines. Every ink is a spell and a key, and as each comes home the world repaints itself around you. Mix all three into WHITE and climb the Grey Tower.

The Vale: a world that fades and repaints

Every colour on the screen goes through one table, which greys out each ink you do not hold yet. Win an ink and the table is rebuilt: the room repaints itself column by column, and every room after it is a little brighter. The inks are keys too: BLUE freezes fords, RED burns thorns, GREEN grows vines.

Fights: the border loads each monster

A fight does not cut to its screen: it loads it, like a tape. Pilot tone in red and cyan, a header, then the picture byte by byte in the Spectrum's own screen order, blue and yellow stripes in the border and the screech of the data with it. Then the colour wheel decides it: a monster hit with its complement takes three times the damage.

The tape that listens: make your apprentice while it loads

Six minutes of loading become the start of the game. The loading screen arrives grey and its colours come home ink by ink; then a page opens beneath it and asks who you are. Type a name, take a keepsake from Orla's shelf, choose the colour of your cloak, while a candle burns down beside the page as the tape loads.

Loading it

Load the tape

Two tapes of the same game. The special one makes your character while it loads, if your emulator plays tapes in real time; the normal one works everywhere, even loading instantly.

Tapes last built: 21 November 2026.

  1. It needs a 128K (a 128K or a grey +2). Pick Tape Loader. On a 48K the tape says it needs a 128K and stops.
  2. Two tapes, one game: pick by how your emulator loads. Once you are playing they are exactly the same.
  3. The special tape, inkwright-special.tap, has the loading page: while it loads you type your name and choose a keepsake and a cloak, with a candle burning down beside you. The Spectrum's own loader can't do that, so the game reads the tape itself, and the tape has to play in real time, the way a cassette does. Use it on a real 128K, or in ZX Spin or Retro Virtual Machine with instant and fast loading switched off. It takes about six minutes.
  4. The normal tape, inkwright-normal.tap, loads the ordinary way, through the Spectrum's own loader, so it works in every emulator, including the ones that load a tape instantly (Fuse with fast loading, phone and web emulators). There is no loading page: when you pick NEW JOURNEY the game asks the same questions on its own screens. Not sure which? Take this one.
  5. Saving: at an inn, start the emulator's tape recording, then say yes. To carry on later, load that tape with Load from tape on the title.
  6. On a real Spectrum: play the tape into the EAR socket from a tape player or a TZX player.

About

How it's made

Our own tools

An assembler and a Spectrum emulator written for this work, in JavaScript. The emulator models the 128K's paging, the AY chip and a tape deck that plays the real pulses.

Checked by playing

The build plays the game from set-up moments: walking, every spell and gate, an altar, a guardian, the inn, a save and a load, a shop, the Grey King and the ending. A search of the world checks it can still be finished after every change to a map.

Then looked at

Every room is drawn to a map and every check leaves a screenshot, because a picture shows what a check can miss.

INKWRIGHT is an original RPG for the 128K ZX Spectrum, written in Z80 assembly with our own assembler and emulator. Made in the UK, by the maker of games for the ZX Spectrum Next, Borders & Loaders and the Spectrum Sound Lab.
